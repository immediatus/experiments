# Falsification Criteria

Each part of the series ends with observations on a real cluster that would refute it, each with the
condition, the prediction and what would count as a refutation. They are predictions: the posts were
written without running them, and nothing here claims they have been confirmed. The full wording is in
each post; anyone with a cluster can test them and report the outcome, refutations included, as
[Reporting a run](#reporting-a-run) describes.

## Reporting a run

This experiment has no cluster kit, so a report states its setup in full. It attaches:

- the Kubernetes version and the configuration of every mechanism involved: the HorizontalPodAutoscaler
  and VerticalPodAutoscaler objects (Part 1), the autoscaler behavior fields and the Cluster Autoscaler
  flags, including `--scale-down-unneeded-time` and `--scan-interval` (Part 2), the API server's request
  timeout and the flow schemas and priority levels the informers fall into (Part 3), and every webhook configuration
  with its failure policy, selectors and match conditions, with any disruption budgets (Part 4), and the names and order of every mutating webhook configuration with each webhook's reinvocation policy (Part 5);
- the trace the criterion names, as exported metrics over the whole run: replica counts and recommendations
  for Part 1; pod readiness, pending pods and node counts with timestamps for Part 2;
  `apiserver_request_total` by verb and resource, `apiserver_flowcontrol_rejected_requests_total` by
  reason and `apiserver_flowcontrol_request_wait_duration_seconds` for Part 3;
  `apiserver_admission_webhook_rejection_count` by error type, replica counts and the drain's eviction responses for Part 4;
  the audit events of the admissions, at the `Metadata` level or higher, with their `mutation.webhook.admission.k8s.io` annotations, and the admitted and dry-run objects for Part 5;
- the trigger, with its start, its length and the share of clients or demand it affected;
- the section of `reproduce.py` whose prediction the run is compared with, and the inputs changed to match
  the cluster, so that the prediction can be recomputed.

A run that cannot meet a criterion's condition, for example because a mechanism cannot be configured as the
criterion requires, is reported as such and leaves the status unchanged.
