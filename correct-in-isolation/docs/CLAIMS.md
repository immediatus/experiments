# Claims and Their Evidence

Every result the series states, with the evidence behind it. **Layer** follows the series: *Bound* is a
proof or a fact read from a source, *Fit* is a reading or an approximation, and *Estimate* is a number from
simulation. **Section** names the part of `reproduce.py` that prints the numbers (`python3 reproduce.py
T12`), **Figure** the drawing made from them, and **Post** the paragraph that states the claim.

A row whose Section reads *proof* is established in the post's text, not by simulation. From the repository
root, `python3 tools/check_docs.py correct-in-isolation` checks that every number in the Statement column is
printed by one of the sections its row cites, rounded at most to the decimals shown. Inputs and numbers
obtained by arithmetic are named in the last column instead, which is not checked.
