# Claims and Their Evidence

Every result the series states, with the evidence behind it. **Layer** follows the series:
*Bound* is a proof or a fact read from source, *Fit* is a reading or an approximation, and
*Estimate* is a number from simulation. **Section** names the part of `reproduce.py` that
prints the numbers (`python3 reproduce.py T12`), **Figure** the drawing made from them, and
**Post** the paragraph that states the claim.

A row whose Section reads *proof* is established in the post's text, not by simulation;
the simulated numbers that support it have a row of their own, tagged *Estimate*.

From the repository root, `python3 tools/check_docs.py capacity-in-arrears` checks that every
number in the Statement column is printed by one of the sections its row cites, rounded at
most to the decimals shown. Inputs and numbers obtained by arithmetic are named in the last
column instead, which is not checked.
